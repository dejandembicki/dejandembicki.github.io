Privacy policy

Last updated: 1 October 2026

This is a personal website. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no advertising, and it never asks you for personal data.

What is stored in your browser

The site remembers your chosen language and theme (light or dark) in your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is never sent anywhere, and you can delete it at any time by clearing the site data in your browser.

Hosting and fonts

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Like any web server, they receive your IP address and basic browser details when the page loads. How they handle this data is described in the privacy policies of GitHub and Google.

Questions

For any question about privacy, use the contact options on the home page.
